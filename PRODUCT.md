# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Independent Indian clinic owners, doctors, receptionists, support staff, and Vachanam operations staff. They work under time pressure on phones and shared clinic computers. Their primary job is to keep calls, appointments, doctor availability, patient follow-ups, WhatsApp conversations, and billing accurate without exposing patient information.

## Product Purpose

Vachanam answers clinic calls in Indian languages, checks live clinic data, books and changes appointments, sends confirmations, and gives each clinic a reliable operational view. Success means fewer missed calls, no false availability claims, no double bookings, and a front desk that can understand the day's work at a glance.

## Brand Personality

Assured, humane, and precise. The experience should feel like a capable clinic team: warm with patients, calm under pressure, and exact about facts.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI SaaS pages with purple gradients, floating glass cards, fake dashboards, or invented metrics.
- Sterile hospital software that feels cold, bureaucratic, or difficult to scan.
- Decorative motion that delays routine clinic work.
- Interfaces that hide patient-safety actions behind ambiguous icons or unfamiliar controls.
- Monochrome templates that make every status and priority look the same.

## Design Principles

1. Make trust visible through clear hierarchy, specific copy, and grounded product evidence.
2. Put the next safe action where clinic staff naturally look for it.
3. Use color to distinguish meaning and priority, never to decorate uncertainty.
4. Keep routine work fast and familiar; reserve expressive motion for the public story and moments of feedback.
5. Preserve patient privacy in every preview, notification, empty state, and analytics surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard navigation, visible focus, 44px touch targets, screen-reader labels, sufficient contrast, zoom-safe layouts, reduced-motion behavior, and color-independent status labels. Mobile layouts must remain usable on low-cost Android devices and slow clinic networks.
