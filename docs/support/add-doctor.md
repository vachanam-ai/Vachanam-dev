title: Adding doctors and staff
audience: clinic
category: onboarding
tags: doctor, staff, add, team
---
To add a doctor, open Settings and go to the Doctors section, then add the
doctor's name and working hours. Vachanam routes each patient to the right
doctor based on the issue and the time they want. Vachanam Voice supports
unlimited doctors in the clinic branch. Receptionists use the mobile app to
mark attendance and manage the queue.
