---
title: Choosing or creating the clinic voice
audience: clinic
category: onboarding
tags: voice, custom voice, clone, recording, upload, language, catalog
---
Every clinic can choose a supported studio voice and call language from the
Voices page. Changing either selection applies from the next call and does not
alter booking rules or patient data.

As a limited founding offer, the first 10 clinics that successfully create or
connect a custom voice receive permanent access to one custom voice. Opening
the page does not consume a place. The owner can record up to 20 seconds,
upload WAV, MP3, M4A, OGG or WebM audio, or paste an existing provider voice
ID. Explicit permission from the speaker is required. A clinic can keep only
one custom voice at a time; delete it before creating a replacement. Once all
10 places are claimed, the custom-voice creation area is hidden for new
clinics, while existing members retain it.
